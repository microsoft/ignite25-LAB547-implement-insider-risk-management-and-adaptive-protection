## 🔥LAB547: Implement Insider Risk Management and Adaptive Protection

*Learn how to use Microsoft Purview Insider Risk Management to detect risky AI behavior and automatically adapt protection based on user activity.*

### Session Description

AI tools like Microsoft Copilot improve productivity but can also expose sensitive data. In this session, you'll use Microsoft Purview Insider Risk Management to detect and respond to risky AI activity. You'll enable analytics, create policies that use AI and DLP signals, and turn on Adaptive Protection to automatically adjust enforcement as risk levels change.

### 🧠 Learning Outcomes

By the end of this session, you'll be able to:

- Enable and configure Insider Risk Management analytics and indicators for AI-related activity
- Create and customize Risky AI usage policies and notice templates
- Integrate Insider Risk Management with DLP and Adaptive Protection to automatically respond to risky AI prompts and responses

### What you'll do

In this lab, you'll:

- Enable analytics and configure insider risk indicators
- Create a Risky AI usage policy
- Add notice templates and assign insider risk levels
- Connect Insider Risk Management with DLP and Adaptive Protection

### 💻 Technologies Used

1. Microsoft Purview Insider Risk Management
1. Microsoft Purview Data Loss Prevention (DLP)
